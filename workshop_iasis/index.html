Scientific content

Generative models have seen spectacular recent advances, to the point where their latest versions are now capable of producing synthetic images and text that are nearly indistinguishable from real content. Among the approaches that have contributed to this progress, diffusion and flow matching methods occupy a central place.

This meeting aims to take stock of recent advances, both fundamental and applied, in the field of generative models, with a particular emphasis on applications in image processing. The call for contributions is open to work on theoretical developments, algorithmic developments, or original applications. A non-exhaustive list of topics includes:

  • generalization of diffusion and flow matching methods
  • training of generative models
  • scaling up generation
  • diffusion and flow matching for discrete data and text
  • connections with optimal transport
  • evaluation of generative models
  • integration of generative models into image processing applications
